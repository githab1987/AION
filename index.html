Financial control, not another ERP.

SPECIAL ALI is designed as a financial control operating system. Accounting, reconciliation, investigation, evidence, taxation, validation and auditability work as connected control domains.

Every important number can answer: “Why?”

Results can be traced to their source, evidence, rule, calculation, validation and execution history. A number without sufficient proof does not silently become truth.

AI assists. The engine decides.

ALI can interpret requests, investigate patterns, form hypotheses, explain results and propose actions. Authoritative state changes remain controlled by backend rules, authorization, validation and human gates where required.

Accounting and tax share evidence — not assumptions.

The same validated source can feed Accounting Core and Tax Core, while each domain applies its own controlled interpretation, rules and validation.

Incomplete but provable beats complete but unsupported.

When evidence, rules or validation are insufficient, SPECIAL ALI can leave a case unresolved rather than fabricate certainty.
